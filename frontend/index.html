ABOUT FUTUREPATH

Helping Malaysians reskill for the AI era.

FuturePath is a career navigator for students, graduates, workers facing restructuring and career switchers. It shows which future-ready careers fit your skills, why, and how to get there with free or low-cost learning.

THE PROBLEM

Work is changing, and people are left guessing

01Jobs are changing faster than people can retrain

AI and automation are reshaping work in Malaysia. MOSTI expects more than 600,000 workers to need reskilling within 3 to 5 years, and clerical roles such as data entry and bookkeeping are declining fastest.

02People use AI, but few are trained for it

56% of Malaysian professionals use AI at work, yet only 26% have had formal training (Hays Asia Salary Guide 2025). Most don't know which of their skills still matter.

03Career advice is generic and hard to trust

Job sites list vacancies, not a path from where you are now. Advice rarely shows its evidence, its date, or how it reached its answer, and paid courses put people off.

HOW FUTUREPATH SOLVES IT

From your CV to a plan you can act on

01 · StartRead your real skills

Upload a CV or fill a short form. Claude picks out skills, each with a quote from your CV, and you confirm every one.

02 · ResultsMatch you to future careers

Careers are ranked by a visible formula: your skills, market demand from dated signals, and optional interest fit. You see each skills gap.

03 · SignalsShow the evidence

Every demand score comes from dated, sourced reports, news and research, refreshed daily: which skills are rising and which jobs are declining.

04 · PathwayPlan an affordable route

An ordered learning plan using checked free, low-cost and HRD Corp-claimable courses, with hours, weeks and a tick-off progress tracker.

05 · ScenariosBe honest about uncertainty

Moderate and Fast AI disruption scenarios show how the picture could change. Results are estimates, not predictions.

HOW WE USE AI

AI supports. Humans decide.

01Fixed, published formulas

AI never decides a score or a career for you.

02Claude only reads and labels

It tags news against our catalog and picks out CV skills. You confirm every skill.

03Checked catalog only

The AI cannot invent jobs, courses or statistics.

AI models used
LLMClaude by Anthropic: tags trend signals, extracts skills from CVs● configured
KEYKeyword matching: used for CV skills and signal tagging when Claude is not configured● keyword fallback

No model scores, ranks or orders anything.
